# Nour · Care Companion 🌿

A gentle, **bilingual (العربية / English)** day-to-day care companion — built to help
you two follow her care plan, track how she's feeling, and stay close through it.

It works on any phone or computer, opens in a browser, and **saves on the device**.
With a shared code, both of you see the same data (sync).

> ⚠️ **Important — please read.**
> This app is a **support and organization tool, not medical advice.** Every
> treatment and dietary decision must come from her **oncology/hematology team and a
> registered dietitian.** Blood-cancer treatment can weaken the immune system, so even
> food choices should be cleared with her team. A fever or new symptom during treatment
> can be serious — **follow the thresholds and instructions her care team gave you, and
> contact them for anything urgent.** The app helps you record and follow *their* plan.

---

## What's inside

- **Today** — a gentle daily checklist (meds, meals, water, rest, mouth care, a calm
  moment…), a water tracker, a quick feeling log, and a "today at a glance" summary so
  whoever opens it can see how her day is going.
- **Health** — log temperature, mood, energy, pain, appetite, sleep, weight, and
  symptoms. A clear **fever alert** appears if a high temperature is logged.
- **Food** — a space for **her care team's dietary rules** (kept at the top), water,
  a meal log, and gentle general food ideas (with safety notes).
- **Care** — medicines (reminder list), appointments, and a shared notes/journal where
  you can leave her loving messages.
- **More** — language, names, water goal, fever threshold, edit the checklist,
  emergency/doctor contacts, and the shared space code.

Everything is bilingual and switches instantly between **Arabic (right-to-left)** and
**English**.

---

## Using it together (shared)

1. One of you opens the app and taps **Create new** — you'll get a short **space code**.
2. Share that code with the other.
3. The other opens the app, taps **Join with code**, and enters it.
4. Now you both see the same checklist, logs, water, notes, and appointments. Changes
   sync automatically (and it keeps working offline, syncing when back online).

The shared data lives in a private backend (Supabase) and is reachable only with your
space code. Keep the code private.

---

## Notes

- **No accounts, no ads, no tracking.** Just the two of you.
- If the internet is down, the app still works on each device and syncs later.
- To change anything — add reminders, edit the checklist, set the doctor's number — use
  the **More** tab and the **Care** tab.

With love, and strength to Nour. 💛
